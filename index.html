About

Research that leaves the lab.

I am a master’s student in Computer Science at Dalhousie University, supervised by Prof. Dr. Frank Rudzicz, and an AI Engineer at pipikwan pêhtâkwan, where I train and serve AI built to corrects unconscious bias or racism directed to Indigenous Peoples in written materials.

Before Canada I did my undergraduate in Computer Science and Engineering at United International University in Dhaka, majoring in Data Science - a perfect 4.00 in the major, Cum Laude overall — and spent a year building production OCR and vision-language systems at Apurba Technologies. I used to also teach at my Alma Mater, part-time, because the questions students ask are the ones worth answering.

My published work runs from tri-modal adversarial attacks on short video to Bangla OCR on documents nobody else would touch. The through-line is the same: several signals, one understanding. During my master's, I am trying to make medical AI understand hierarchy in medical ontologies and help them ACTUALLY see, read, and hear.
