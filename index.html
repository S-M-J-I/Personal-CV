About Me

I am an AI Researcher and Part-time Faculty member in the Department of Computer Science & Engineering at United International University (UIU). Previously, I worked as a Software Engineer in the core Machine Learning team at Apurba Technologies Ltd., where I focused on building scalable ML services and VLM-based OCR systems.

My primary research interests lie at the intersection of Multi-modal Learning and Multimodal AI Safety. Specifically, I am interested in:

  • Multi-modal Representation Learning: Aligning representations across vision, text, and audio.
  • Multi-modal AI Safety & Alignment: Evaluating and defending against adversarial threats on video/multimodal media.
  • VLM-based Document Processing: Developing advanced architectures for Bengali Optical Character Recognition and low-resource LLM pipelines.
